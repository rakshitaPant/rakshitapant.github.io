Core Data Platform Exit & Modernization

A regional credit union faced a hard contract deadline with 1,600 pieces of undocumented transformation logic and 185 dashboards at risk.

Cost reduction
50 to 60%
Dashboard load time
30% faster
Read the full case study

Problem. The client's legacy BI contract was expiring on a hard date. 1,600 queries of transformation logic had no documentation, and 185 dashboards used daily by sales and marketing were at risk of going dark.

Approach. I ran the exit as a structured program: built a governed star schema model, directly migrated half the transformation logic to the new warehouse while coaching the client's team through the rest, then led the dashboard migration under a shared design and governance framework.

Outcome. 100% of owned scope delivered on deadline, 50 to 60% lower platform licensing costs, and a 30% cut in dashboard load times with zero disruption to business reporting.
